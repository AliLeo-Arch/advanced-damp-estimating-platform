Build a Job Estimating & Quoting Tool for UK Damp Proofing Contractor
Fixed-price - Expert - Est. Budget: $800
Only freelancers located in the United Kingdom may apply.
Advanced Damp Ltd is a specialist damp proofing and structural waterproofing contractor working across London and the South East. Our surveyors carry out on site surveys and produce written reports and quotations. We currently price jobs manually, which is slow, inconsistent between surveyors, and makes margin hard to control. A job estimating tool that takes survey measurements as input and outputs a priced job with a controlled margin. The tool must price the following work types: Chemical DPC injection and replastering (linear metres of wall, height of replaster, number of walls) Cavity drain membrane systems and boarding (m² of wall and floor, membrane spec, fixings, battens, boarding) Sump and pump installations (fixed price packages, twin pump and battery backup options) Timber treatment (m² or linear metres, joist and floor works) Condensation and ventilation (extractor units, PIV, per unit installed) For each, it needs to handle: Materials, with a rate table I can update in one place Labour, by day rate or by output rate per m²/linear metre Waste and skip costs Access and preliminaries Travel by distance band from the office Target margin applied by job type, with the ability to override and see the effect on margin immediately A minimum job value floor Output A single priced summary showing cost, margin percentage, margin value and sell price A customer facing quotation in our branding, exportable to PDF A saved record of every estimate so we can compare quoted vs actual later Platform Open to either an advanced Excel or Google Sheets build, or a simple web app. Tell us in your proposal which you would build and why, given the tool must be used on site and edited by a non technical business owner. Who we are looking for Demonstrable experience estimating UK construction, ideally remedial treatment, waterproofing, damp proofing, or a related trade Strong spreadsheet modelling, or full stack development if proposing a web build Able to structure rate tables so a non technical user can maintain them without breaking the model Comfortable working from a discovery call plus our existing quotes and price lists
